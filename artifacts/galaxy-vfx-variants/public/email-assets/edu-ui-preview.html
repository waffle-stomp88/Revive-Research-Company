BPC-157: Tissue Repair Mechanisms
How Body Protection Compound modulates angiogenesis, growth factor signaling, and nitric oxide pathways in connective tissue repair.
Beginner Friendly
What Makes BPC-157 Special
Think of your body's healing system like a construction crew. BPC-157 acts like a project manager — coordinating repair cells, bringing in the right materials, and keeping recovery on schedule.
  • Derived from a protein naturally found in gastric juice
  • Supports formation of new blood vessels at injury sites
  • Works with your body's natural repair signals — doesn't override them
Healing System Tissue Repair
Mechanism of Action
BPC-157 activates VEGFR-2 receptors to stimulate angiogenesis and neovascularization at injury sites. It modulates the NO-synthase system and upregulates EGF and FGF-2, coordinating the full tissue repair cascade.
