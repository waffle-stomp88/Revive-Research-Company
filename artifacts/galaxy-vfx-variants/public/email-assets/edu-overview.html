BPC-157: Tissue Repair Mechanisms
How Body Protection Compound modulates angiogenesis, growth factor signaling, and nitric oxide pathways in connective tissue repair.

What Makes BPC-157 Special?
Think of BPC-157 as your body's emergency construction crew. Where normal healing might take weeks, BPC-157 signals cells to start building and repairing immediately — activating multiple repair pathways simultaneously.
  • Activates VEGF pathways to stimulate new blood vessel growth at injury sites
  • Upregulates growth hormone receptors in damaged connective tissue
  • Modulates nitric oxide signaling for cytoprotection and anti-inflammation
