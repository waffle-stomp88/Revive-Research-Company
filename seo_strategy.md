# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Product listing and product detail pages
- Research stack pages
- Education and guide pages
- Public tools and informational pages
- Public XML/text discovery files such as `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and future `llms.txt`

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard routes under `/dashboard/**`
- Admin routes under `/admin/**`
- Checkout, cart, account, and other transactional/private flows unless they leak indexable SEO signals onto public pages

- Researchers and professionals looking for premium peptide research compounds and education
