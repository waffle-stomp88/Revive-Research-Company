DO NOT PUT THESE ON YOUR SITEMAP
1. ANY product pages

This is non-negotiable.

That includes:

/products

/product/*

/peptides

/shop

/cart

/checkout

/bundles

/protocols

/buy-*

Why:

You explicitly do not want public visibility

Google indexing = screenshots + scraping + regulators + platform reviewers

Email providers do look at indexed content when reviewing accounts

If it requires login, password, or approval, it does NOT belong on a sitemap.

2. Any gated or account-based pages

Examples:

/login

/signup

/register

/dashboard

/profile

/account

/members

/academy/* (if inside login)

/admin (obviously)

Why:

Google cannot authenticate

It creates crawl errors

It looks sloppy and unprofessional

It signals “this site doesn’t understand access control”

3. Research Academy internal content

Even if educational.

Examples:

Lesson pages

Course modules

PDFs

Video lesson URLs

Protocol breakdowns

Why:

You want this behind profile-based access

You don’t want fragmented content appearing out of context in search

It weakens your authority narrative (Google seeing “random peptide lessons”)

The landing page is fine.
The content itself is not.

4. Anything referencing Replit

Absolutely never again.

If your sitemap contains:

*.replit.app

old staging URLs

dev environments

That is an instant trust hit.

You already saw Google reject this — correctly.

5. Utility / system pages

Examples:

/privacy (optional — usually not needed)

/terms

/cookies

/404

/reset-password

/verify-email

Why:
They add zero SEO value and clutter Google’s crawl budget.

6. Pages you’re “not ready for yet”

This is subtle but important.

If you look at a page and think:

“Eh… we’ll clean that up later”

It does NOT belong on the sitemap.

Sitemaps are intentional, not exhaustive.

✅ WHAT SHOULD BE ON YOUR SITEMAP (SMALL LIST)

For Revive Research right now, this should be very short:

✅ Include only:

Homepage
/

About / Mission
/about or /mission

Research Academy landing page only
/research-academy
(NOT the internal lessons)

Any purely informational, public-facing trust page
(no peptides, no purchase intent)

That’s it.

If your sitemap has more than ~5–10 URLs right now, it’s wrong.
