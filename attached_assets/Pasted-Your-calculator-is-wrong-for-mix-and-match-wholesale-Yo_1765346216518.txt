Your calculator is wrong for mix-and-match wholesale.

You’re showing:

bulk discount applied to a single total

no per-SKU wholesale protection

one universal discount %

This works ONLY IF:

👉 your cost per vial is the same across peptides
👉 your margins are the same on every peptide

But we both know they’re not.

If you keep this calculator, customers will expect:

“I bought 300 vials, so everything should be 25% off.”

Which can destroy your margins on expensive peptides like Retatrutide/IGF-1 LR3/etc.

You need to adjust the calculator so it displays:

“up to X% off depending on SKU”

OR show wholesale pricing after they submit an application

OR show tier unlocks but not actual final pricing

Right now, the calculator promises a blanket discount you cannot sustainably offer.

Discounts vary by compound based on manufacturing cost.
