## What's actually happening

The Galaxy page (`/galaxy`) is your Synergy Engine visualization — that constellation/star-map view of peptides. That's a **heavy 3D/canvas component**, almost certainly using `three.js` or `react-three-fiber`. Loading those libraries in dev mode through Vite means:

1. Vite has to transform every dependency on first request
2. Three.js + react-three are massive (thousands of modules)
3. The Replit dev proxy adds latency to every chunk request
4. While that's happening, you stare at white or "Loading..."

The fact that **navigation goes white again** when you click another page tells me the same thing is happening for other heavy routes — Vite re-transforming, re-fetching, re-bundling on demand.

## Why this got worse recently (the actual answer)

The reconstitution wizard added new heavy deps (`jspdf`, `qrcode`, possibly `framer-motion` if it was newly added). When Vite sees new heavy deps, it triggers a **dep re-optimization** that invalidates cached chunks and forces re-transformation of related modules. That's the "this used to work fine, now it doesn't" pattern.
