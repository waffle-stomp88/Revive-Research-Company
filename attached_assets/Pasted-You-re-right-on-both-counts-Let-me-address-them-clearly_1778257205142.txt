## Problem 1 — Internal scrollbar on the right column

You don't want the right column to have its own scrollbar. You want it to scroll WITH the page, but stay anchored relative to your viewport when scrolling.

That's a different sticky behavior than what was applied. The current implementation has `max-h-[calc(100vh-6rem)] overflow-y-auto` which forces internal scrolling when the column gets tall.

What you want: just `position: sticky` + `top: 80px` + `align-self: start`. No `max-h`, no `overflow-y: auto`. The column attaches to your viewport at 80px from top and slides naturally with the page.

The tradeoff: if your right column is taller than the viewport (5+ items, lots of upsells), the bottom of it (including the Pay button) will be cut off below the viewport. You'd have to scroll past the bottom of the page to see it.

That tradeoff matters for our current concern about "Pay button always accessible" — but we can solve that differently (a sticky bottom bar for desktop, or just trust that most carts won't be that tall).
