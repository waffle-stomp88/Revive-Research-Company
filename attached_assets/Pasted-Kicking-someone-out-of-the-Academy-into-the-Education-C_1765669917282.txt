Kicking someone out of the Academy into the Education Center breaks flow, weakens the product, and signals “this is just links,” not a system.

Here’s the clean, scalable way to structure this—and it solves all of your concerns without boxing you in later.

The correct mental model (anchor this)

Think of the Academy as the guided experience
and the Education Center as the reference library.

Same knowledge. Different delivery.

They should share content, not compete for it.

The winning structure (hybrid, not either/or)
1. Academy = Curated, Sequenced, Opinionated

Lessons are embedded inside the Academy UI

Content is:

chunked

paced

contextualized

Includes:

checkpoints

summaries

progress tracking

XP / achievements

No outbound navigation during lessons

User stays inside the Academy. Always.

This is where learning happens.

2. Education Center = Ungated, Flat, Reference-Only

Same core articles exist here

But:

no XP

no progress

no sequencing

no personalization

Treated like:

a wiki

a glossary

a library

This is where people look things up, not learn deeply.

How to technically connect them (important)
Single Source of Truth (SSOT)

Each topic exists once in your database.

Then you render it differently:

Academy view

embedded

lesson framing

checkpoints enabled

progress tracked

Education Center view

clean article

no lesson framing

no progression

no XP

Same content. Two skins.

What happens when someone clicks a lesson?
❌ What you have now (bad)

Academy → kicks to Education Center
→ user feels lost
→ experience feels duct-taped

✅ What you want

Academy → embedded lesson view
→ sidebar stays
→ progress updates
→ next lesson CTA appears

No context switching.

What about people who don’t want the Academy?

They still get value.

Here’s the tradeoff (and it’s intentional):

Education Center users get:

access to information

zero friction

zero guidance

Academy users get:

structure

sequencing

comprehension

confidence

You’re not withholding knowledge.
You’re withholding guidance.

That’s fair. And defensible.

How this cleans up your Education Center (big win)

Once you do this:

Education Center becomes:

leaner

searchable

non-duplicative

Academy becomes:

immersive

intentional

sticky

You eliminate:

redundant navigation

repeated CTAs

cognitive clutter

One subtle but powerful addition

On Education Center articles, add a small, non-pushy banner:

“Want a guided walkthrough of this topic?
This article is part of the Research Fundamentals Academy.”

That’s it. No selling. Just orientation.

Final recommendation (clear and direct)

Do not:

make articles Academy-only

force people into courses to read

keep bouncing users between systems

Do:

embed Academy lessons natively

keep Education Center open and flat

use the Academy for structure, not access control

This preserves:

openness

trust

UX quality

future optionality
