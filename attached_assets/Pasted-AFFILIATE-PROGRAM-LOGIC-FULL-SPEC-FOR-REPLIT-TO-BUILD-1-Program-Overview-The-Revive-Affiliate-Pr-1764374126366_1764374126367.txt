AFFILIATE PROGRAM LOGIC — FULL SPEC FOR REPLIT TO BUILD
1. Program Overview

The Revive Affiliate Program is a two-tier commission system:

Tier 1 (Direct Sales)

Affiliates earn X% commission on every sale they personally generate.
(Example: 20% — you can define the exact number.)

Tier 2 (Influencer Override Commission)

If an affiliate recruits another affiliate, they become that new affiliate’s upline.

The upline affiliate earns 10% of the commissions generated by the affiliate they recruited.

This is not 10% of total sales, but 10% of the DIRECT commission that the recruited affiliate earns.

This creates a two-tier earning system:

Tier 1 → Commission from your own sales

Tier 2 → Commission override from your team’s sales

There are no commissions beyond Tier 2.

✅ 2. Sign-Up Flow

Step 1: Visitor submits application (name, email, payout method).
Step 2: System generates a unique referral link for the new affiliate:
reviveresearch.co/?ref=AFFILIATE_ID

Step 3: If they were recruited by another affiliate:

The upline affiliate ID is stored in the database.

This creates the parent → child relationship for Tier 2 earnings.

✅ 3. Tracking Logic

Whenever a sale occurs:

A. Detect the referral

System reads the ?ref= parameter attached to the customer’s purchase.

Assigns the sale to the corresponding Affiliate_ID.

B. Log the sale

Store:

Order ID

Customer name (optional, depending on privacy)

Sale amount

Affiliate_ID who made the sale

Timestamp

C. Calculate Tier 1 Commission

Example:

Sale total: $100

Affiliate commission rate: 20%

Tier 1 commission = $20

D. Check if the affiliate has an upline

If affiliate.upline_id exists:

Calculate Tier 2 override:
upline_commission = direct_affiliate_commission * 0.10

Example:

Direct affiliate earns $20

Upline earns 10% of $20 = $2

E. Save all commission records

Store:

Affiliate earning $20

Upline earning $2

The affiliate has an upline

✅ 8. Recruitment System Logic

When Affiliate A recruits Affiliate B:

Affiliate B signs up using Affiliate A’s referral link:
reviveresearch.co/affiliates?ref=A

System stores:
B.upline_id = A.affiliate_id

Now A receives 10% override on:

Every commission B generates

Forever

As long as both accounts remain active

There are no deeper levels
(A → B → C does NOT give A commissions from C.)

How Our 2-Tier Affiliate Program Works

You earn money in two ways:

1. Direct Sales — Your Own Customers

Whenever someone buys using your link, you earn a direct commission (for example, 20%).

2. Team Sales — Affiliates You Recruit

When you refer someone to join as an affiliate, they become part of your team.

Every time they make a sale, you earn a 10% override on the commission they earn.

Example:

Your teammate earns $30 from a sale

You earn $3 override

This is called a two-tier affiliate structure — simple, powerful, and ethical.

There are no extra levels, no MLM complexity.
