Pack selector needs a visual revision. Here's what to change:

Remove the "Sign in" text from every pack row. Just show a lock icon where the price would go — no text. Having "Sign in" repeated four times is noise. The "Sign in to see pricing" button above the pack selector already handles that CTA.
Give the selected row a slightly lighter background (try #1a1a22 or similar) in addition to the neon border. Right now the only difference between selected and unselected is the border color, which is too subtle. The selected row should feel elevated.
Add a radio indicator on the left side of each row — empty circle for unselected, filled neon circle for selected. This gives a clear visual cue of which pack is active. See the mockup image for reference.
The "Popular" badge on the 5-pack needs to sit inside the row's border, not floating outside it. Anchor it top-right inside the card padding.
In the order summary, replace "Sign in to see total" with just the lock icon and "Sign in" as a clickable link — keep it minimal, same pattern as the price area above.
Bring back a quantity stepper for the single vial option only. If someone selects "1 vial" they should be able to adjust quantity (1, 2, 3, etc.). Multi-packs (3, 5, 10) are fixed quantities — no stepper needed for those. When "1 vial" is selected, show the +/- stepper. When a multi-pack is selected, hide it.
The overall section needs more vertical spacing between rows — add 6-8px gap between pack options. Right now they're stacked too tight and read like a form, not a selection.
