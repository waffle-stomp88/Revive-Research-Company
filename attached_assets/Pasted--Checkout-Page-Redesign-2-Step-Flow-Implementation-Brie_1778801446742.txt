# Checkout Page Redesign — 2-Step Flow Implementation Brief

**Priority:** High — ship before launch
**Goal:** Replace the current checkout layout with a 2-step linear flow (Shipping → Payment) that eliminates back-and-forth scrolling and places the Pay button at the bottom of the experience

---

## Why 2 Steps

Stripe's analysis of millions of checkout sessions found that for transactions above $200, multi-step checkout completes at 82.4% vs 78.1% for single-page. Our average order will exceed $200. The reason: completing Step 1 (shipping) creates psychological investment through the "endowment effect" — once someone has entered their info, they're more likely to finish.

However, each additional step loses 10-20% of users. A 3-step flow (shipping → payment → review) adds a redundant review page. The solution: 2 steps with the review built into Step 2. This gives us the commitment benefit without the extra drop-off point.

Additional data points driving this design:
- 66% of customers want checkout completed in 4 minutes or less
- Reducing form fields from 12-15 to 7-9 cuts abandonment by 8-12%
- Mobile checkout converts at 1.53% vs desktop 3.36% — the gap is caused by UX friction, not intent
- Single-page checkout reduces mobile abandonment by 20-30% vs multi-PAGE flows, but 2-step (not multi-page) with progress indicator performs best for high-AOV purchases

---

## Current Problems Being Solved

1. **PAY button appears ABOVE the shipping form on mobile.** Customer sees a pay button they can't use, scrolls past it to fill in address, then has to scroll back up to pay.

2. **Full product images and BAC water upsell in checkout.** These are cart behaviors leaking into checkout. The customer already reviewed their order — showing it again with images pushes form fields below the fold.

3. **Order summary duplicated at full size.** The complete order summary with product images takes up an entire screen on mobile before the customer can start entering information.

4. **No clear visual progression.** Customer doesn't know how many steps are left or where they are in the process.

---

## Step 1: Shipping

### Layout (top to bottom):

**Progress Bar**
- 2-step horizontal indicator: "1 Shipping" (active, cyan) → "2 Pay" (pending, gray)
- Clean, minimal, always visible at the top below the nav

**Compact Order Summary Bar**
- Single line: product name, dosage, quantity, and total price
- Example: "TB-500 · 10mg × 10 — $600"
- Dark background bar, not a card — takes up minimal vertical space
- NO product image in this bar

**Trust Strip**
- One line below the summary bar: "COA included · Same-day shipping · Batch verified"
- Green dots, small text — this is the competitive defense element (customers at checkout often have competitor tabs open)

**Saved Address Card (if address exists in profile)**
- Green-tinted card showing their saved address
- Green checkmark icon + "Saved Address" label
- Full address displayed
- "Edit" link in cyan to modify
- "Use a different address" collapsed option below

**If no saved address:** Show the full address form instead:
- Full Name *
- Email * (pre-filled from account)
- Phone (optional, labeled "Recommended for shipping updates")
- Street Address *
- City * / State * / ZIP * (on one row)

**Shipping Method Selector**
- Standard (free, same-day if before 12pm CT) — pre-selected, green border
- Express ($15, 1-2 business days)
- Radio button style, same visual pattern as the pack selector on product pages

**CTA Button**
- "Continue to Payment →"
- Full-width, neon green
- This is the ONLY primary action on this page

**Trust Badges (below CTA)**
- SSL encrypted · Data protected · No spam

---

## Step 2: Payment & Review

### Layout (top to bottom):

**Progress Bar**
- Step 1 shows green checkmark (completed)
- Step 2 active (cyan)
- "← Back" link in the nav returns to Step 1

**Compact Order Summary Bar**
- Same product line as Step 1
- Now also shows "Shipping to [City, State]" to confirm their info carried over

**Trust Strip**
- Same as Step 1

**Payment Method Selector**
- Credit/Debit (labeled "RECOMMENDED" with cyan badge) — pre-selected
- PayPal
- ACH
- Horizontal row of three cards, same visual style as pack selector

**Card Fields (when Credit/Debit selected)**
- Card Number (with small "VISA MC AMEX" text right-aligned)
- Expiry / CVC (side by side on one row)
- Name on Card

**PayPal (when PayPal selected)**
- Replace card fields with a single "Pay with PayPal" button that redirects to PayPal

**ACH (when ACH selected)**
- Replace card fields with Link Money / ACH flow as implemented

**Order Review Card**
- Small product thumbnail (36px), product name, dosage, pack info, price — one row
- Subtotal
- Shipping (Standard — FREE or Express — $15)
- Tax (calculated from Step 1 address)
- Divider
- **Total** — large, neon green, prominent

**PAY Button**
- "🔒 Pay $[total]"
- Full-width, neon green, 14px font, 14px padding — the most prominent element on the page
- This is the LAST element before the footer content
- Must be visible after filling in payment fields without scrolling past anything else

**Terms Line**
- "By completing this purchase you agree to our Terms of Service and Refund Policy"
- Small text, centered, with cyan links

**Trust Badges**
- SSL encrypted · PCI compliant · Secure payment

**RUO Disclaimer**
- Red-tinted card: "Research Use Only — Not for human or animal consumption"

## Technical Implementation Notes

### State Management
- Step 1 data (shipping address, method) persists when moving to Step 2
- "← Back" from Step 2 returns to Step 1 with all fields preserved (not cleared)
- URL can optionally update: `/checkout` (Step 1) → `/checkout/payment` (Step 2), or handle with internal state — either works

### Pre-filling
- Email: always pre-fill from the logged-in user's account
- Name: pre-fill from account profile if available
- Address: if user has a saved address (from previous order or profile), show the Saved Address Card instead of empty form fields
- Phone: leave empty but show as optional

### Validation
- Step 1: Validate all required fields before allowing "Continue to Payment"
- Step 2: Validate payment fields before allowing "Pay"
- Show inline field-level errors, not a generic alert

### Tax Calculation
- Tax should calculate when the user completes Step 1 (we now have their address)
- Show "Tax calculated at checkout" in the cart page
- Show the actual tax amount in Step 2's Order Review card

### Mobile Behavior
- Both steps are full-width single-column layouts
- The progress bar is sticky at the top (below the nav) so the customer always knows where they are
- The compact order summary bar scrolls with the content (not sticky — save vertical space)

### BAC Water Upsell
- Do NOT show the BAC water upsell in checkout — it belongs on the cart page only
- Checkout is for completing the purchase, not adding items

### "Missing Something?" Prompt
- If the cart contains peptides but no bacteriostatic water, and the user has NOT dismissed this prompt on the cart page, consider showing a small non-intrusive reminder at the top of Step 1 (above the address form): "You have peptides in your cart but no BAC water. Add one?" with a quick-add button
- This is optional and should be dismissible — do NOT block the checkout flow

---

## What to Remove from Current Checkout

1. **Full-size product images** — replace with the compact summary bar (product name + quantity + total only)
2. **BAC water upsell card** — move to cart page only
3. **Full order summary appearing before the shipping form** — order summary moves to Step 2, after payment fields
4. **PAY button appearing above the shipping form** — PAY button is now the last element on Step 2

---

## What NOT to Change

- Payment processing logic (PayPal, ACH, credit card)
- Order creation flow on the server
- Email confirmation templates
- Tax calculation logic (just move where it displays)
- The logged-in user verification
