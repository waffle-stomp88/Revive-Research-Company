## Issues worth surfacing

**1. The redirect to `/systems/{slug}` from the existing system guide cards needs more thought.**

#370 Step 3 says: update the existing "Healing" and "Hormonal" system guide cards to point to `/systems/healing` and `/systems/hormonal` instead of `/guides/healing-peptides` and `/guides/hormonal-peptides`.

That's the right end state, but it leaves a question: what happens to `/guides/healing-peptides` and `/guides/hormonal-peptides` as URLs?

Three options:
- **Leave them live as-is** — they become orphan content competing with the new hub for the same query. Bad for SEO (cannibalization).
- **301 redirect to `/systems/{slug}`** — clean, transfers any backlinks/authority. But you lose a URL that's already indexed and ranking.
- **Keep them live as a "Deep Dive" subpage linked from the hub** — preserves indexed URLs and any authority they've built, but you need to ensure they don't compete with the hub. Add a canonical from the deep-dive page to the hub, or differentiate the content explicitly.

The brief mentions option 3 ("they become 'deep dive' content within the hub") but doesn't specify the canonical/SEO handling. Worth deciding before deploy. **My recommendation: keep them live, link from the hub, add `<link rel="canonical" href="/systems/{slug}">` on the deep-dive pages** so Google consolidates ranking signals on the hub. The deep-dive content becomes secondary detail, not a competing page.

**2. The "Browse by Body System" placement decision is doing two things at once.**

Step 2 says place it inside the Peptides tab content. But Task 1.1 already creates 7 system hubs that aren't peptide profiles — they're a tier above. Putting "Browse by Body System" *inside* the Peptides tab signals to users that systems are a sub-category of peptide profiles, when structurally they're a parent.

Two cleaner options:
- **Place it above the tabs entirely** (as a hero-adjacent section, before the tabbed interface starts). This signals "systems are the primary navigation; tabs are a secondary view."
- **Place it as the first section of the page**, with the tabs below it. Same effect.

Putting it inside one tab buries it from anyone landing on a different tab and undersells its importance. This is essentially a partial Phase 2 move — and that's fine, but it should be a deliberate choice.

**3. Counting consistency between hub and landing.**

With ~100 peptides × 7 systems × every page render, it's not catastrophic, but a `useMemo` (or computing once at module load) is cheap insurance. Worth a one-line note in the ticket.
